What we collect

Account details, delivery addresses, order history, messages sent through chat, and technical logs (IP address, browser) for security.

How we use it

To process orders, show your tracking, prevent fraud, and improve the service through aggregated analytics.

Sharing

Sellers see what they need to fulfil your order. We never sell personal data.

Activity and error tracking

To keep the service working we record which pages signed-in members open, searches, cart and order steps, and technical errors (with the page, browser and account involved). Support staff and administrators may view this trail to fix a problem you report; every such look is itself logged. We skip page-view tracking when your browser sends Do-Not-Track or Global Privacy Control, and delete old activity after about six months.

Seller and staff records

Seller verification documents are stored privately and seen only by the seller and Nyota reviewers. Staff attendance and payroll records are visible to the staff member, and to the people who prepare and approve payroll.

Your rights

You can view, correct or request deletion of your data by contacting support through the chat in the footer.
