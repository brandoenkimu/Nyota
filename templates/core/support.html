Support

Most answers are below. For anything else, our team replies by email.

Do I need an account to browse?

You can read about {{ COMPANY_NAME }} and what it offers without one. Browsing shops, buying and tracking an order all need a free account, so every order is tied to someone and can be traced if anything goes wrong.

How do I track an order?

Sign in and open "Track an order" from your account, or use the order number and email from your confirmation message.

What payment methods are supported?

M-Pesa, card payments, and cash on delivery where the seller offers it. Every payment is confirmed instantly and recorded on your order.

How do I become a seller?

Use "Open a shop" to sign up as a seller. You'll go through a short verification step before your shop goes live, to keep the marketplace trustworthy for buyers.

I forgot my password.

Use "Forgot password" on the sign-in page for your account type (customer, seller or staff). A reset link is emailed to the address on file.

Something looks wrong or I suspect a scam listing.

Please contact us straight away with the shop or product name. Reports like this are reviewed by our verification team, not left to sit in a queue.
